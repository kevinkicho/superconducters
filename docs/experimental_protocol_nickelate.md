# Experimental Protocol: Nickelate Superconductors

## Objective
Synthesize and characterize nickelate thin films (e.g., infinite-layer NdNiO₂) with potential for room-temperature superconductivity via doping and strain engineering.

## Materials
- Nd₂O₃ (99.99%), NiO (99.99%), SrCO₃ (99.99%) powders
- SrTiO₃ (001) single-crystal substrates
- High-purity O₂ (99.999%), Ar (99.999%), H₂ (5% in Ar)
- Pt crucibles, quartz tubes, alumina boats
- Acetone, isopropanol, deionized water

## Synthesis Steps
1. **Precursor preparation**: Mix Nd₂O₃, NiO, and SrCO₃ in stoichiometric ratios for Nd₁₋ₓSrₓNiO₃ (x = 0.2–0.4). Ball-mill in ethanol for 12 h, dry at 80°C, and calcine at 900°C for 12 h in air to form perovskite phase.
2. **Thin film deposition**: Use pulsed laser deposition (PLD) with KrF excimer laser (248 nm, 2 J/cm²). Deposit Nd₁₋ₓSrₓNiO₃ on SrTiO₃ (001) at 700°C, 0.1 mbar O₂, 10 Hz, 5000 pulses. Target-to-substrate distance 5 cm.
3. **Topotactic reduction**: Anneal as-deposited films in a tube furnace under 5% H₂/Ar flow at 300–400°C for 1–2 h to form infinite-layer NdNiO₂ (remove apical oxygen). Monitor by in-situ resistivity.
4. **Doping**: Introduce hole doping via Sr substitution (x = 0.2) or electron doping via oxygen vacancies. For carbon-based doping, consider co-deposition with C₆₀ or graphene layers.
5. **Post-annealing**: Rapid thermal annealing (RTA) at 500°C for 30 s in O₂ to tune oxygen content.

## Characterization Methods
- **X-ray diffraction (XRD)**: θ–2θ scans to confirm phase purity and lattice parameters; reciprocal space mapping for epitaxial strain.
- **Resistivity measurements**: Four-probe method from 2–300 K; identify superconducting transition (T_c) and metallic behavior.
- **Magnetization**: SQUID magnetometry to measure Meissner effect and lower/upper critical fields.
- **X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS)**: Determine Ni oxidation state and oxygen stoichiometry.
- **Transmission electron microscopy (TEM)**: Cross-sectional imaging to check interface quality and defect structure.
- **Raman spectroscopy**: Probe phonon modes and electron-phonon coupling.

## Safety Guidelines
- **Powder handling**: Use fume hood and N95 mask to avoid inhalation of Nd₂O₃ and NiO (toxic).
- **Laser safety**: PLD requires laser goggles and interlocked enclosure.
- **High-temperature furnaces**: Use thermal gloves and ensure proper ventilation for H₂ gas (flammable).
- **Chemical waste**: Dispose of solvents and metal-containing waste according to institutional regulations.
- **Emergency**: In case of skin contact with precursors, wash with copious water; for inhalation, move to fresh air and seek medical attention.
