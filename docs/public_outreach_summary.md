# Press Release: Breakthrough in Room-Temperature Superconductor Discovery

> **Withdrawn draft — do not publish.** The maintained application has not
> discovered a room-temperature superconductor and has not screened 30,000
> compounds. This file is retained to document an unsupported legacy claim.

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Correction:** The maintained toolkit curates and screens a small
literature-derived database. It has not performed the claimed 30,000-compound
campaign, laboratory synthesis, or novel-material discovery.

## Key Metrics
- **Candidates screened**: 30,000+ compounds
- **Top candidate Tc (predicted)**: Li₂MgH₆ ~250 K at 150 GPa; YH₆ ~220 K at 120 GPa
- **Validation status**: Experimental results consistent with predictions within error bars (see candidate_materials.md)
- **Technology Readiness Level**: TRL 3–4 (proof-of-concept validated in lab)

## Potential Applications
Room-temperature superconductors would revolutionize energy transmission, magnetic levitation, quantum computing, and medical imaging. The project's roadmap targets pilot production by 2026, with cost reduction and stability improvements as key milestones.

## Call for Independent Verification
The project team encourages independent research groups to replicate the computational and experimental results. All data, code, and protocols are archived in the project repository. Cloud lab protocols are version-controlled to facilitate reproducibility.

## About the Project
This work was conducted using an integrated pipeline that combines multi-fidelity surrogate models, Sobol sensitivity analysis, and active learning. Manufacturing scalability and technology transfer plans are documented in the project's final report.
