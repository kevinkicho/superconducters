# Executive Summary: Room-Temperature Superconductor Discovery & Manufacturing

## Top Candidate Compound: LaH10 (Lanthanum Decahydride)

**Predicted Tc:** ~250–260 K (DFT/Machine Learning, 2017–2019)
**Experimental Tc:** ~250 K at 170 GPa (Drozdov et al., Nature 2019)
**Pressure Requirement:** ~150–200 GPa (diamond anvil cell)
**Manufacturing Cost Estimate:** Extremely high (>$10M/kg) due to extreme pressure synthesis, limited scalability.
**TRL/MRL Level:** TRL 2–3 (concept & experimental proof-of-principle); MRL 1–2 (basic materials feasibility).

## Key Risks
- **Pressure stability:** LaH10 is only stable above ~150 GPa; no ambient-pressure retention.
- **Scalability:** Diamond anvil cells cannot produce bulk quantities.
- **Cost:** Current synthesis is prohibitively expensive for commercial use.
- **Reproducibility:** Some experimental results have been contested; need independent verification.

## Recommended Next Steps for Commercialization
1. **Explore lower-pressure hydrides** (e.g., CSHx, YH6, LiHx) that may stabilize at <50 GPa.
2. **Develop advanced synthesis techniques** (e.g., laser-heated diamond anvil, multi-anvil press) to increase yield.
3. **Investigate doping & strain engineering** to reduce required pressure while maintaining high Tc.
4. **Establish a cloud-lab pipeline** for high-throughput screening of candidate materials.
5. **Engage with national labs** (e.g., NIST, DOE) for large-scale pressure equipment access.
