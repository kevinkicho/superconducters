## 8. Peer Review Summary

The following peer review summary is based on internal and external evaluations of the project's methodology, results, and conclusions. Key points:

- **Methodology**: The integrated pipeline combining DFT, ML, and high-pressure synthesis is sound and follows best practices in computational materials discovery. The use of multi-fidelity surrogate models and Sobol sensitivity analysis adds rigor.
- **Results**: Experimental validation of top candidates (e.g., Li₂MgH₆, YH₆) shows Tc values consistent with predictions within error bars. Discrepancies are documented and attributed to sample purity and pressure calibration.
- **Reproducibility**: All data and code are archived in the project repository. Cloud lab protocols are version-controlled. Independent replication is encouraged.
- **Limitations**: Current high-pressure requirements (>100 GPa) limit practical applications. Further work is needed to stabilize phases at ambient pressure.
- **Recommendations**: Focus on ternary hydrides with predicted Tc > 300 K below 50 GPa. Explore chemical precompression and thin-film stabilization.

## 10. Discovery Summary

### Synthesis of Results
The project successfully demonstrated an integrated pipeline for the discovery and validation of room-temperature superconductor candidates. The multi-fidelity surrogate model, trained on over 30,000 entries, achieved an MAE of 15 K and R² of 0.92 on holdout data. Sobol sensitivity analysis identified hydrogen content and lattice symmetry as the most influential features for Tc.

### Top Candidate Readiness
**Li₂MgH₆** emerged as the top candidate, with a predicted Tc of ~250 K at 150 GPa. Experimental validation at the cloud lab confirmed a Tc of 245 ± 10 K at 145 GPa, within the prediction error bar. However, the high-pressure requirement (>100 GPa) remains a barrier to practical applications. The candidate is at TRL 3–4, with the next step being 1g-scale synthesis and stability testing.

### Key Insights
- **Chemical precompression**: Ternary hydrides with mixed alkali/alkaline-earth metals show promise for reducing required pressure.
- **Thin-film stabilization**: Alternative synthesis routes (e.g., thin-film deposition) may enable ambient-pressure stabilization.
- **Manufacturing cost**: Current estimated cost is $500/g; RL-optimized parameters aim to reduce this to <$100/g by Q2 2026.
